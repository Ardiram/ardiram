## Research interests

- **Computational electrochemistry:** modelling electrochemical reactions and processes at electrified solid–liquid interfaces.
- **Atomistic modelling:** using density functional theory (DFT) and ab initio molecular dynamics (AIMD) to investigate materials and reaction mechanisms.
- **Electrocatalysis:** understanding catalyst materials, reaction pathways, activity and selectivity, particularly for energy-related transformations.
